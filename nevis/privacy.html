Nevis Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 September 2026

Nevis is a workout tracking application that allows users to record and monitor strength and cardio training.

Information stored by Nevis

Nevis stores information that you enter into the app, such as workouts, exercises, sets, repetitions, weights, cardio sessions, distances, times, notes, and training goals.

This information is primarily stored locally on your device using the app's local database.

Nevis does not sell your personal information.

Google Drive backup

Nevis provides an optional Google Drive backup and restore feature.

If you choose to connect Google Drive, Nevis requests permission to access files created or used by Nevis for the purpose of backing up and restoring your workout database.

When you create a backup, a copy of your workout database is uploaded to your Google Drive account. When you restore a backup, Nevis downloads that database from your Google Drive account.

Nevis uses this access only to provide the backup and restore functionality.

You can disconnect Google Drive from within the app. Disconnecting revokes Nevis's Google Drive authorization. Files already stored in your Google Drive account may remain there until you delete them.

Google services

Google Drive functionality is provided using Google services. When you choose to use Google Drive backup, your use of Google Drive is also subject to Google's own privacy policy and terms.

Analytics and advertising

Nevis does not currently use third-party advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting services.

Data sharing

Nevis does not sell or share your workout data with advertisers or other third parties.

Data is transferred to Google only when you choose to use the optional Google Drive backup functionality.

Data retention and deletion

Workout data stored locally remains on your device until you delete it through the app, clear the app's data, or uninstall the app.

Google Drive backups remain in your Google Drive account until they are replaced or deleted.

You can remove local data by deleting it within Nevis or by clearing or uninstalling the app. You can remove backed-up data through Google Drive.

Security

Nevis uses standard Android and Google authentication mechanisms when accessing Google Drive. Google Drive access tokens are used only when required to perform authorised backup and restore operations.

Children's privacy

Nevis is not specifically directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

This privacy policy may be updated as Nevis develops. Changes will be reflected on this page together with the updated date.
